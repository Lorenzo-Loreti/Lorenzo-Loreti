# Ciao! 👋 I'm Lorenzo Loreti (aka Lanci)

### 🎓 Double Master's Degree Student in Automation Engineering & Engineering Cybernetics

I am an engineering student currently pursuing a double degree at **UNIBO** (University of Bologna) and **UniStuttgart** (University of Stuttgart). Born in Imola, Italy, I currently split my time between Stuttgart and my hometown.
